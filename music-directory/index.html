Meet Our Members

Who We Are

♩

Brotherhood

A tight-knit community of musicians bonded by a shared passion for performance, education, and service. We uplift each other musically and personally.

♬

Heritage

Founded over a century ago, our chapter carries a rich tradition of musical excellence, artistic integrity, and leadership in the arts.

♫

Diversity

Our members span every genre and instrument — from classical orchestral to jazz, musical theatre, and contemporary production. All voices belong here.

What We Do

01

Performances

We host recitals, ensemble concerts, and open-mic showcases throughout the academic year, giving every member a stage.

02

Community Service

From music therapy outreach to free youth workshops, we bring the power of music to underserved communities around campus.

03

Professional Growth

Masterclasses, guest artist panels, and networking events prepare our members for careers across the music industry.

04

Social Events

Bonfire jam sessions, formal galas, and brotherhood retreats keep our chapter connected beyond the rehearsal room.
