Who We Are

A brotherhood of musicians united by tradition, artistry, and a commitment to advancing music in America.

Our Story

A Legacy of Music & Brotherhood

Phi Mu Alpha Sinfonia was founded on October 6, 1898 at the New England Conservatory of Music in Boston, Massachusetts. Since then, it has grown to become the oldest and largest secret national fraternity in music in America.

The Beta Tau Chapter carries forward this legacy with a deep commitment to the advancement of music, fellowship among musicians, and mutual support. Our brothers represent every corner of the musical world — from classical and jazz to composition, education, and beyond.

Our Pillars

What Defines Us

♩

Brotherhood

A bond forged through music that extends beyond the rehearsal room. We support, challenge, and inspire one another as musicians and as men.

Heritage

Over 120 years of tradition. Our rituals, ideals, and standards have shaped generations of music professionals across the nation.

♫

Every instrument, every genre, every voice. From orchestral performance to music production — all disciplines are represented and valued.

Our Values

The Object of This Fraternity

The object of this Fraternity shall be for the development of the best and truest fraternal spirit; the mutual welfare and brotherhood of musical students; the advancement of music in America and a loyalty to the Alma Mater.

— Ossian Everett Mills, Founder
