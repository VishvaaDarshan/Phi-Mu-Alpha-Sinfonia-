Our Music

Fraternity Songs

01

The Red and Black

Official Hymn

Hail to thee, our noble Order,
Mighty Phi Mu Alpha,
Red and Black our colors flying,
Ever, ever nigh.

Brothers joined in sacred union,
Pledged to music's art,
Bound by ties that none can sever,
Heart to loyal heart.

02

Hail Sinfonia

Gathering Song

Hail Sinfonia, hail to thee,
Loyal sons we'll always be.
Through the years our voices raise,
Singing ever songs of praise.

Red and black our banners fly,
Music lifts our spirits high.
Brothers all, from shore to shore,
Phi Mu Alpha evermore.

03

The Toast Song

Brotherhood Anthem

Here's to our fraternity,
Noble Phi Mu Alpha,
May she ever prosper,
May she live for aye.

Raise your voices, brothers dear,
Sing with all your might,
For the red and black we love,
Burning ever bright.
